  • Alfred Zaccagnino

    President & CEO

  • Phil Leseur

    Legal Advisor

    In 2010, Phil Leseur joined Samarian Group, bringing his wealth of legal expertise to the team. His educational journey began at Queen's University in Kingston, Ontario, where he pursued his undergraduate degree and earned a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) with a major in Political Studies. He then returned to his hometown, Victoria, British Columbia, to attend law school at the University of Victoria, where he obtained his law degree.

    Phil embarked on his legal career at the prestigious Vancouver law firm of Russell & DuMoulin, now part of the Canadian national law firm Faskens. His practice primarily focused on corporate and real estate law, and he played a significant role in transformative projects like the Coal Harbour and Yaletown developments, which reshaped Vancouver's downtown core.

    In 2004, Phil transitioned to in-house counsel for the Bear Mountain partnership, overseeing all legal matters related to the Bear Mountain real estate and resort development in Victoria, British Columbia. This project marked the largest of its kind in Western Canada.

    In 2008, Phil took on the monumental task of acquiring the National Hockey franchise, the Tampa Bay Lightning. He represented the buying group, collaborating closely with the law firm Weil Gotshal & Manges LLP's Dallas office, who served as lead counsel for the buying group. Phil managed the legal team working with the majority partner of the buying group and liaised with legal representatives of the National Hockey League and the financing entity, Galatioto Sports Partners. His work extended into 2010 when he collaborated with the New York office of the law firm Hiscock & Barclay (now Barclay Damon LLP) and lead counsel Bob Lanza for the sale of the Tampa Bay Lightning, followed by arbitration before NHL Commissioner Gary Bettman.

    In 2011, Phil returned to a full-time role at the Bear Mountain development. He initially worked with the Court Appointed Monitor and later with an HSBC subsidiary company, which assumed control and ownership of the Bear Mountain real estate development and resort project. His focus was on formulating a 'workout' plan for the redevelopment and sale of the Bear Mountain project. In 2013, he became a consultant for Ecoasis, which had real estate projects in Hawaii and Whistler, British Columbia, and acquired the Bear Mountain real estate and resort development from the HSBC subsidiary.

    As the Legal Advisor for The Samarian Group of Companies, Phil plays a pivotal role in advising on corporate structure, tax efficiency, compliance, financing documentation, and contractual drafting.

  • Wolfgang Heimerl

  • Major General John F. Wharton

    U.S. Army, Retired

    Major General John F. Wharton (U.S. Army Ret.) has dedicated over three decades to serving the Nation, retiring with extensive experience in leadership, technology, innovation, and logistics. He currently advises both public and private sector organizations on national security and technology acceleration, and is a Partner at One Defense, an initiative aimed at advancing defense technology through collaboration among DOD stakeholders, industry, startups, venture capital, and academia.

    Previously, General Wharton was the Commanding General of the United States Army Research, Development and Engineering Command, leading 13,800 personnel and managing 75% of the Army's R&D budget. His achievements include winning and leading five of the seven President's National Manufacturing Innovative Institutes and developing international science and technology roadmaps. His prior roles include Commanding General of the U.S. Army Sustainment Command and Chief of Staff at the U.S. Army Materiel Command, among others. General Wharton holds degrees from the United States Military Academy and the Naval War College and has served as a Visiting Senior Research Fellow at the National Defense University.
